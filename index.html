Collaborative Projects

Capstone, coursework and club projects. Each one opens up with what we were asked to do, what we ended up building, and how it turned out. Reports and CAD packages are attached where I can share them.

APS380 | EV Design

AWD EV Demonstration Platform

Built for APS380. A scaled dual-motor platform, so that drivetrain layout and torque split were things you could actually watch instead of just read about.

Capstone | UTWind

Low-Speed PMSG for Vertical Axis Wind Turbine

My Capstone. We designed a custom direct-drive generator for UTWind's 2026 turbine, which had to make power at low RPM without being difficult to start.

MIE540 | Product Design

The WindRunner

Our MIE540 project. A small propeller-driven car that had to cover a set distance as fast as possible while still being safe and cheap enough to sell.

MIE301 | Mechanism Design

Drone-Mounted Sampling Arm

For MIE301 we were given a sampling arm built for a Europa rover and had to adapt it for Io. We ended up mounting it on a drone instead.

MIE346 | PCB Design

Analog Signal Conditioning PCB

For MIE346 we had to design and build a board that scales a 0 to 0.35 V sensor signal up to 0.25 to 3.3 V and filters the noise out of it.

MIE315 | Design for the Environment

Google Home Housing: Wood vs Plastic

For MIE315 we looked at whether Google should switch their smart speaker housings from plastic to wood, and had to back the answer up with real numbers.

MIE243 | Reverse Engineering

Product Dissections and 12:1 Gearbox

Throughout MIE243 we did a series of Dissections, which meant taking apart a real device and then modelling it in SolidWorks. The 3D model higher up this page came out of this project.

MIE243 | Machine Design

Desktop Pick and Place Machine

The Term Project for MIE243. A three-axis SMD placement machine specified to ±0.01 in and costed all the way down to a real bill of materials.

APS112 | Design for a Client

DMAIC Teaching Device

Designed for a Master's Professor teaching Six Sigma. A two-variable device to replace the three-variable catapult his students kept getting lost in.

About Me


My Journey

What pulled me into engineering was seeing an idea go from concept to reality. I like the stretch from CAD to prototypes, testing, and the small revisions that make something finally feel right.

Focus Areas

I am most drawn to mechatronics, manufacturing, EV systems, and product development. I like work where design, fabrication limits, performance, and team coordination all matter at once.

Creative Pursuits

Photography, writing, and cinematography are where I slow down and notice more. They sharpen the same instincts I use in engineering: composition, patience, detail, and how the finished thing will land with someone else.
