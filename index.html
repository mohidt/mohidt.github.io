Technical Skills & Engineering Approach

My engineering skill set encompasses a blend of analytical problem-solving, programming, and computer-aided design. I have developed proficiency in MATLAB and Python for data analysis and engineering simulations, allowing me to model and optimize complex systems.

Additionally, I am skilled in AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and Onshape, which I have used to design and prototype mechanical components. My experience with Microsoft Office Suite supports effective documentation, data management, and reporting.

Whether I’m analyzing datasets, refining CAD models, or troubleshooting design issues, I approach challenges with a methodical and results-driven mindset.

Internship Experience & Key Takeaways

My internships at AGS Automotive Systems and Parts Apart have provided me with hands-on experience in both manufacturing engineering and product design.

At AGS, I focused on optimizing production processes, improving efficiency by 15%, and gaining a deep understanding of how design decisions impact large-scale manufacturing.

At Parts Apart, I was immersed in the full product development cycle, from market research to prototyping, refining CAD models, and collaborating closely with stakeholders.

Together, these experiences have strengthened my ability to bridge design and real-world application, balance innovation with feasibility, and work within cross-functional teams to develop practical engineering solutions.

University & Personal Projects

Beyond my professional experience, I have engaged in hands-on projects that further developed my engineering skill set. In the Engineering Strategies and Practice II course at the University of Toronto, I collaborated with a team to develop a functional prototype for a real-world client, ensuring clear documentation and effective communication throughout the design process.

Collaborative Projects

Robotic Arm for Rover - MIE301

We reimagined a sample-collecting robotic arm for Io, Jupiter’s most volcanic moon, shifting from a bulky land-based rover to a lightweight, drone-mounted mechanism.

Printed Circuit Board - MIE346

Developed and fabricated a PCB-based power supply to scale and filter a low-voltage analog signal using op-amps and low-pass filters.

Google Home Housing - MIE315

Consulted on replacing plastic housings with wood for Google Home speakers, evaluating cost, sustainability, and social responsibility.

Dissections - MIE243

Hands-on mechanical reverse engineering project — taking apart devices, studying their components, and modeling them in SolidWorks.

Pick and Place CNC Machine – MIE243

Designed a desktop CNC machine to automate PCB component placement — emphasizing accuracy, modularity, and affordability.

Experimental Design Device – APS112

Developed a hands-on two-variable device for the Six Sigma Green Belt course to help students learn DMAIC through physical experimentation.
